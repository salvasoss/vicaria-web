# Vicaria Web

Responsive product catalog and ordering website developed for **Vicaria**, an Argentine automotive products brand.

The project was built to provide the company with a modern digital presence, allowing customers to explore its products, review product information and place orders through WhatsApp.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://vicaria.com.ar

---

## About the project

Vicaria Web was developed as a real-world business solution focused on simplifying the product inquiry and ordering process.

The website includes a product catalog, individual product pages, shopping cart functionality and different purchase flows for retail and wholesale customers.

The main objective was to create a simple and responsive user experience while adapting the application to the company's commercial requirements.

---

## Main Features

- Responsive product catalog
- Individual product detail pages
- Shopping cart
- Product quantity management
- Automatic order total calculation
- WhatsApp ordering integration
- Retail and wholesale purchase flows
- Wholesale order requirements
- Contact and inquiry forms
- Form validation
- Responsive navigation
- Mobile-friendly interface
- Reusable React components
- Dynamic product information

---

## Wholesale Orders

The application includes a dedicated wholesale flow.

Customers interested in purchasing larger quantities can access specific wholesale information and submit their order details through a validated form.

The system also handles commercial rules based on the quantity of products requested and redirects the customer to WhatsApp to coordinate pricing, shipping and order details.

---

## Shopping Cart

Users can add products to the cart, modify quantities and review their order before contacting the company.

The generated WhatsApp message automatically includes the relevant order information, making the purchase process easier for both the customer and the business.
